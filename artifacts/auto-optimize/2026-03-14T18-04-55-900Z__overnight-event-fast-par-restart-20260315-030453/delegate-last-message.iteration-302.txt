1. What changed

The only change was to the fast benchmark `wallMs` normalization in the worker-backed paths so an explicit `wallMs=0` is now honored instead of being forced to 100 ms.

Updated:
[engine-fast-worker.js](/C:/Users/suzuk/AppData/Local/Temp/fact-sim-auto-patch/1773522058211-25960/js/app/engine-fast-worker.js#L275)
[engine-fast-par-worker.js](/C:/Users/suzuk/AppData/Local/Temp/fact-sim-auto-patch/1773522058211-25960/js/app/engine-fast-par-worker.js#L398)
[engine-fast-par-host.js](/C:/Users/suzuk/AppData/Local/Temp/fact-sim-auto-patch/1773522058211-25960/js/app/engine-fast-par-host.js#L678)

Each path now does:
`Number.isFinite(requestedWallMs) ? Math.max(0, requestedWallMs) : 2000`

That keeps `dt` and `event` untouched and preserves the default 2000 ms when `wallMs` is missing or invalid.
