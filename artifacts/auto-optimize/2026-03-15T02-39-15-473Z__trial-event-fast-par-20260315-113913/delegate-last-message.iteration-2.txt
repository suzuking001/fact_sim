Performance hypothesis: `event-fast-par` was paying avoidable host-side overhead in `_stepParallel` by flattening all emitted messages and preallocating/scanning every partition bucket on each substep/flush round. Cutting those transient arrays and empty buckets should reduce GC and dispatch overhead when cross-partition traffic is nontrivial.
